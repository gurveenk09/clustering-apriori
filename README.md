
# Analysis of Unicorn companies using Clustering & Apriori
## Executive Summary
A unicorn company is a privately held company with a current valuation of over USD 1 billion. Using Python were able to:
1. Segment unicorn companies based on GDP per capita, Global Innovation Index, Total Funds Raised and Number of Patents by Country.
2. Analyse investor collaboration patterns using association rule mining

This code would be useful for anyone looking to optimise their portfolio with unicorns filing for IPOs. For investors, this serves as a medium for region based collaboration opportunities.


## Methodology

1. Python for the exploratory data analysis, clustering and apriori
2. PowerBI dashboard to understand trends and region-wise distribution of unicorns for investors

## Skills

1. Python: Pandas, Matplotlib, Numpy, Seaborn, Plotly, Mlxtend, Scikit-learn
2. Power BI: Dax, writing functions, ETL, calculated columns, data visualization, data modeling

## Results & Business Recommendation:

A dashboard would enable users to track concentration of Unicorns by regions, providing a visual guide to users to track changes, view trending industries, top investors, number of unicorns each year, geographic concentration. This analysis showed us:
1. 82% of the valuation came from 4 countries: US, China, India and UK
2. 50% of the companies became unicorns in 2021
3. 33% of the total investment came from 10 investors
4. 20% unicorns comapnies were Fintechs.

I would like to make the following recommendations for any investor or people looking to invest in future IPOs of these unicorns:
1. Optimising Portfolios & identifying opportunities: Mitigating risks based on proven collaboration strategies. Frequent collaboration among investors in the start-up ecosystem could open doors for upcoming investors.
2. Leading Economic and Innovation Trends: The presence of unicorns could serve as a leading indicator of future economic and innovation trends in a country.
3. Region-based Collaboration Opportunities: Exploring regions with similar economic and innovation profiles for potential investor collaborations.
